Master Product & Functional Specification

⸻

1. PRODUCT VISION

Office Manager AI is an intelligent operating system designed for audit and assurance firms.

It connects:

* people;
* engagements;
* audit methodology;
* documents;
* emails;
* working papers;
* review processes;
* quality management;
* performance management;
* billing;
* AI assistance;
* institutional knowledge.

The objective is not to replace auditors or professional judgment.

The objective is to create a single operating environment capable of answering, at any time:

1. What engagements are currently active?
2. Where is each engagement in its lifecycle?
3. Who is working on each engagement?
4. Are the right people assigned?
5. What is missing?
6. What documentation has been received?
7. What audit risks remain insufficiently addressed?
8. Which Working Papers still require review?
9. Which review points remain unresolved?
10. Who must act next?
11. Which engagements are at risk?
12. Which employees need support or training?
13. What is the quality of the firm’s work?
14. What is the financial position of each engagement?
15. Can every important decision be traced back to evidence?

Office Manager AI is therefore not just:

* a document management tool;
* a chatbot;
* a CRM;
* an audit software package;
* a workflow tool;
* a performance dashboard.

It is the operating layer connecting all of them.

3. SYSTEM PHILOSOPHY

Office Manager AI follows six principles.

Principle 1 — Evidence before assumption

A filename is not evidence.

A folder name is not evidence.

A database flag is not necessarily evidence.

The system must inspect the underlying source whenever a professional conclusion depends on it.

⸻

Principle 2 — Humans retain professional judgment

AI can:

* propose;
* detect;
* compare;
* summarize;
* challenge;
* recommend;
* prepare.

Humans retain authority for:

* client acceptance;
* independence decisions;
* materiality;
* significant judgments;
* audit conclusions;
* review sign-offs;
* audit opinion;
* report issuance.

⸻

Principle 3 — One source of operational truth

The application must avoid parallel, contradictory records.

Structured data, documents and communications remain connected.

⸻

Principle 4 — No invisible work

Every important action should be traceable.

The system should be able to answer:

Why was this decision made?

⸻

Principle 5 — AI does not equal authority

The fact that an AI recommends an action does not mean that action has been approved.

⸻

Principle 6 — Audit workflow should reflect actual audit work

Auditors should still be able to work with:

* Excel;
* Word;
* PDFs;
* scanned evidence;
* photographs;
* client files.

The platform enhances that work rather than forcing auditors into an artificial environment.

13. EXPECTED POPULATION LOGIC

Example:

PBC request:

All December bank statements

The client has five bank accounts.

Expected population:

1. Bank A
2. Bank B
3. Bank C
4. Bank D
5. Bank E

Only Bank A is received.

The correct status is:

PARTIAL — 1/5

not:

RECEIVED COMPLETE

⸻

14. AUDITOR CONFIRMATION LOOP

If Mission Controller cannot determine the expected population confidently, it asks the auditor.

Example:

I identified five bank accounts from the trial balance.
Please confirm whether this is the complete bank population.

The auditor can:

* Confirm;
* Modify;
* Add missing items.

This improves PBC accuracy.
